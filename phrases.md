# İstinye Park: phrase list (draft 2)

Draft 2: Baba's answers filled in (San Diego, 2nd grade, green, chocolate) and Grandma's pet names added.

How to read it:

- **They say** is what a character says to Noah. A slash means the same question asked another way.
- **He says** is the right reply. A dot (·) separates replies that are both right.
- Wrong choices are not written here. The game uses right replies from other rows as the wrong choices, so everything he hears is real Turkish.
- I used "sen" everywhere, because the goal is family. Say so if you want "siz" for the shop characters.

To correct: edit this file, or just tell me the row number and the fix.

## What Grandma calls him

**yavrum, canım, bir tanem.** The older characters drop these into their questions the way she does, so he hears them inside real sentences:

- Nasılsın yavrum?
- Aç mısın canım?
- Hoş geldin bir tanem!
- Güle güle yavrum!

They are added to the spoken question only. The right reply stays the same.

## The replay button

The button that repeats a question is labelled, and says out loud, **"Tekrar söyler misin?"** (can you say that again). He learns the most useful phrase of all by pressing it.
